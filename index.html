The residence

A home on a Longboat Key canal, looking at the water through mangroves. One of the most striking houses in the community.

There are streets on Longboat Key you do not find by accident. Bayou Sound runs through the gated Bay Isles community, where neighbors know each other by name and unfamiliar cars do not appear. The house stands directly on the canal with its own private dock, and the canal leads out to Sarasota Bay and the Gulf.

Generous volumes, clerestory light and a siting that makes the house one of the most distinctive on the street. Inside, three levels open toward the water: silver in the morning, gold in the evening.

Hurricane impact-rated windows and doors, a smooth stucco facade, a kitchen in stone, fully tiled bathrooms, terraces with glass railings. A house that looks as if it had always stood here, and works to the standard of today.
